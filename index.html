Dopamine-Supportive Diet

Berries (Blueberries & Strawberries): Rich in polyphenols that guard against neural oxidative fatigue.
Dark Leafy Greens: Folate-rich spinach and kale aid gastrointestinal regularity.
Omega-3s (Walnuts & Flaxseed): Bolsters myelin nerve insulation and memory recall.

Medication Timing Rule

Protein Separation: Levodopa medication competes with dietary proteins (meat, eggs, cheese) for gut absorption. Take Levodopa 45 minutes before or 1 hour after heavy protein meals.
Evening Rest: Avoid caffeinated tea past 4 PM to prevent nocturnal motor restlessness.
